Stellar Portfolio Dashboard

Aggregate multiple Stellar wallets and review balances with XLM USD pricing from CoinGecko.

Aggregate and visualize multiple Stellar wallets in one view.

How it works

  • Paste one or more Stellar wallet addresses.
  • The app fetches balances from Stellar Horizon.
  • XLM balances are priced in USD using CoinGecko.

Use cases

  • Track multi-wallet treasury in one place.
  • Share portfolio snapshots with your team.
  • Monitor XLM exposure and USD value quickly.

Example treasury view

Open a prefilled dashboard with a URL like ?wallets=GABC...,GDEF... and the app will auto-load those wallets.
