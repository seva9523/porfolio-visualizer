Quick Read
This tool runs hypothetical goal scenarios using the inputs you provide. It is educational only and does not provide financial advice.
How to use
1) Click Add Financial Goal Simulation
2) Enter goal amount, timeline, savings, and contributions
3) Review the probability meter and forecast chart (hypothetical)
Inputs (plain language)
Target Amount: the number you want to reach
Time Horizon: how many years you have
Monthly Contribution: what you add each month
Return / Volatility: scenario assumptions (not predictions)
Disclosure
Educational use only. Simulations are hypothetical and not predictive. Past performance does not guarantee future results. This is not investment, tax, or legal advice.
