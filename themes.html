👋 What is a Market Theme?

A market theme is a group of companies connected by a big idea — like Artificial Intelligence, Clean Energy, or Dividend Income. Think of it like a trend or category in the economy.

What can you do here?

  • 🔍 See which ideas have been doing well recently
  • 📊 Understand how risky or stable each theme is
  • ⚖️ Compare different themes side by side
  • 🧪 See what adding a theme might do to your portfolio

This is for learning and exploration — not investment advice.

💡 Good to know: Many investors build a core portfolio first (broad stock and bond funds). Themes are often used as additional ideas to explore alongside a diversified foundation.
