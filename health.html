What this checks

This dashboard is intentionally simple. It’s meant to help you *understand* characteristics—not tell you what to do.
Concentration
How much of the portfolio is dominated by the top positions (HHI + top weight).
Historical turbulence
Annualized volatility and max drawdown based on daily closing prices.
Growth summary
CAGR (compound annual growth rate) over the selected period, purely descriptive.
